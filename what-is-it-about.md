# What is the Personal Learning Dashboard Agent?

## What is it about?

The Personal Learning Dashboard Agent is a simple, private learning
system for one person, in any field.

Instead of a fixed course, a feed of endless articles, or lessons
chosen for everyone, it gives you **one real article at a time**,
chosen for the topics you want to learn, with everything you need
to actually understand it and remember it — all in one dashboard
that you open when you have time.

It works for a designer, a lawyer, a doctor, an engineer, or anyone
else who wants to keep learning in their own field. You tell it your
field and your topics, and it builds your dashboard around them.
Each person gets their own copy, starting empty, with their own
settings, lessons, and notes. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

## How does it work?

1. **You choose what to learn.** You give your field, your topics,
   and how long you want each session to be (15 to 90 minutes).
2. **The agent finds one real article.** It researches current
   public topics in your field and chooses one real, verified
   external article, with its full link and source name. It never
   invents a link, and it never uses your private notes as an article.
3. **You learn it, inside the dashboard.** The article opens inside
   the dashboard, not in an outside browser. Each lesson has:
   - A summary of what the article is about
   - What you should remember from it
   - The full link to read it in full
   - A worked example, so you can see it used practically
   - A required quiz and exercise — the quiz is a self-check with
     a tick-box, not auto-graded
4. **You take notes, by topic.** Notes are filed under their topic
   and kept together in one place. When you save a note, the article
   it came from is automatically attached as **Proof** — a linked
   article title with its source — so you can always trace a note
   back to where you learned it. You can Edit or Delete any note,
   and the button to save a note is called **Save note**.
5. **You ask when you are stuck.** Select any text in an article
   and ask for an explanation in Explain This, or add it as a note.
   Explanations are saved with the lesson.
6. **You keep going, at your pace.** Add new topics any time in
   Settings, and they join the rotation. Past and planned lessons
   are recorded in Article Logs. If you start a note and leave
   without saving — even if you switch to Explain This — your draft
   is saved and restored exactly when you come back.

## How will it be helpful?

- **It removes the "what should I learn today?" problem.** You do
  not have to search, filter, or decide from scratch every day.
  One clear item, chosen for your topics, is waiting for you.
- **It fits a busy life.** You choose when to learn and for how
  long. A session can be 15 minutes or 90 minutes, and the
  dashboard is there when you have time, not on a fixed schedule
  you have to keep up with.
- **It helps you actually remember, not just read.** Every lesson
  ends with an example, a quiz, and an exercise, so you practise
  the idea instead of only scrolling past it.
- **Your notes stay useful.** Because notes are organised by topic
  and each one carries its Proof article link, you can look back
  months later and still know where an idea came from and why
  you wrote it down.
- **It respects your time and attention.** One item at a time,
  no feed, no clutter, and no auto-generated future lessons or
  difficulty predictions pretending to know what you need next.
  Topics you choose drive what comes next.
- **It works in any field.** A lawyer can learn contract law,
  case analysis, and legal writing. A doctor can learn new
  clinical guidance. An engineer can learn new tools and methods.
  The system stays the same; only the field and the articles change.
- **It stays private.** Your dashboard, lessons, notes, topics,
  and settings belong to you alone. Sharing the agent with someone
  else gives them the same system, not your learning.

## Who is it for?

- Anyone returning to work or study after a break who wants a
  structured, practical way to catch up.
- Anyone changing fields, or keeping up with fast-changing work,
  who needs current, real articles — not made-up summaries.
- Anyone who takes notes but loses them, or cannot remember where
  an idea came from.
- Anyone who wants to learn a little, regularly, rather than
  bingeing a course and forgetting it.

## In one sentence

One real article at a time, in your field, with a summary, an
example, a quiz and an exercise, notes you can trace back to their
source, and a dashboard that waits for you when you have time.
