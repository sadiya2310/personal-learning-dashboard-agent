# Personal Learning Dashboard — Claude Version

A field-neutral version of the Personal Learning Dashboard, adapted
for Claude. Paste the prompt below into Claude to get your own
private copy, in your own field. Nothing is shared with anyone else,
and it starts empty.

---

## Paste this into Claude

> Build me a personal learning dashboard as one interactive
> artifact I can open and use again. It is for one person only —
> me — in my field, starting empty with my own settings.
>
> First, ask me for my field, my level, the topics I want to learn,
> and how long I want each session to be (15 to 90 minutes,
> default 30). Do not assume my field or topics.
>
> Build the dashboard with these tabs, in this order:
> Article, Article Logs, Notes, Explain This, Settings.
>
> **Article tab — one lesson at a time, opening inside the
> dashboard, never in an outside browser:**
> - One real, verified external article, with its full link and
>   source name. Never invent a link, title, date, or quote.
>   Never use my private notes as an article.
> - A summary of what the article is about.
> - What I should remember from it.
> - The full link to read it in full.
> - A worked example or use case.
> - A required Quiz & Exercise block at the end. The quiz is a
>   self-check with a tick-box, not auto-graded.
> - Add Notes and Ask AI only in the top section, beside
>   "Your lesson now" — nowhere else.
>
> **Article Logs tab:**
> - "Add an article, video, etc." at the top, with the lesson
>   list directly below it, in date order.
> - Logs record past and planned lessons. They do not
>   auto-generate future lessons, and there is no difficulty
>   level or prediction of future lessons.
>
> **Notes tab:**
> - All notes combined in one place, filed by topic, defaulting
>   to the current topic.
> - While drafting a note, show no proof picker, proof tag, or
>   link section in the form.
> - The submit button must be named **Save note**.
> - On saving, automatically attach the current article as
>   **Proof** — its title, source name, and full link, copied
>   from the lesson's stored link.
> - On a saved note, show the Proof pill and linked article
>   title read-only. No Change proof action after saving.
> - Every note has **Edit** beside **Delete**. Editing opens
>   the saved text with a topic picker; Save changes updates
>   it, Cancel restores the saved text, and the Proof link
>   stays attached while editing.
> - If I start a note and leave without saving — including
>   switching to Explain This — save the draft and restore it
>   exactly when I come back. It clears only when I save the
>   note or discard the draft.
>
> **Explain This tab:**
> - Its own tab. Selecting any text in an article offers two
>   options: Ask AI (opens Explain This with the selected text
>   ready) and Add Notes (opens Notes with the selected text
>   quoted under the current topic).
> - Save each explanation with its lesson.
>
> **Settings tab:**
> - Show only This week and What you're learning, including a
>   "+ Add a new topic" form. New topics join the rotation and
>   appear in the article, notes, and learning-window pickers.
> - Let me add recurring learning windows (days, start time,
>   duration, optional topic).
>
> **How I will use it in Claude:**
> - Keep all lessons, notes, and settings in this one artifact,
>   saved in my browser so they are there when I reopen it.
> - Add an Export button so I can download my notes and lessons
>   as a backup, and an Import button so I can restore them.
> - Each day I open this chat, I will ask you for the next
>   lesson in my rotation, and you will add that real, verified
>   article to the dashboard in the same format.
>
> Keep the design clean and simple: one item at a time, plain
> language, mobile-friendly, and no clutter.

---

## How to use it in Claude

1. Open Claude and start a new chat.
2. Paste the prompt above.
3. Answer Claude's questions: your field, your topics, and your
   session length. For example: "I'm a lawyer. Topics: contract
   law, case analysis, and legal writing. 30 minutes."
4. Claude will build your dashboard as an artifact. Open it and
   use it from there.
5. Each time you want a new lesson, ask Claude in the same chat
   for the next lesson in your rotation, and it will add it to
   the dashboard in the same format.
6. Use Export regularly to back up your notes and lessons, so
   you do not lose them if you clear your browser data.

## What is different from the Muse version?

- **It is rebuilt for Claude, not transferred.** The Muse
  dashboard cannot be imported into Claude. This version is
  built from the same instructions, with the same flow, tab
  order, and button names.
- **Storage is in your browser, not in a Muse database.**
  Lessons and notes are saved where you open the artifact,
  so use Export as a backup, and keep using the same browser
  and device when you can.
- **Daily lessons are asked for, not scheduled.** In Muse,
  a daily lesson can arrive at a set time. In Claude, you ask
  for the next lesson when you are ready to learn, and Claude
  adds it.
- **Everything else is the same:** one real article at a time,
  summary, what to remember, worked example, quiz and exercise,
  notes by topic with a Proof link, Edit beside Delete,
  Save note, Explain This, and your own topics and settings —
  private to you, in any field.
