# Personal Learning Dashboard Agent

One real article at a time, in your field, with a summary, an example, a quiz and an exercise, notes you can trace back to their source, and a dashboard that waits for you when you have time.

## What is it?

A simple, private learning system for one person, in any field. Instead of a fixed course or an endless feed, it gives you one real, verified article at a time, chosen for the topics you want to learn, with everything you need to actually understand it and remember it — all in one dashboard that you open when you have time.

It works for a designer, a lawyer, a doctor, an engineer, or anyone else who wants to keep learning in their own field. Each person gets their own copy, starting empty, with their own settings, lessons, and notes. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

## How it works

1. **You choose what to learn.** Your field, your topics, and how long each session should be (15 to 90 minutes).
2. **The agent finds one real article.** A real, verified external article, with its full link and source name. It never invents a link, and it never uses your private notes as an article.
3. **You learn it, inside the dashboard.** Each lesson has a summary, what to remember, the full link, a worked example, and a required quiz and exercise.
4. **You take notes, by topic.** Notes are filed under their topic, and each one automatically carries its source article as Proof, so you can always trace a note back to where you learned it.
5. **You ask when you are stuck.** Select any text in an article and ask for an explanation, or add it as a note.
6. **You keep going, at your pace.** Add new topics any time; past and planned lessons are recorded in Article Logs.

## Privacy

Sharing this agent gives someone the same system, not your learning. Their dashboard starts empty — your lessons, notes, topics, and settings are never included.
